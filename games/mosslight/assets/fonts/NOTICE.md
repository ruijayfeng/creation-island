with fontTools for this game's interface. Licensed under SIL Open Font License
1.1; see OFL.txt. Original copyright and license records remain in the font.

Original font: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssc
Source file: NotoSansSC[wght].ttf
Rebuild: tools/subset_font.py

CreationTitles.ttf is the unmodified full Noto Sans SC variable font from the
same upstream source, used for user-supplied project and achievement titles.
It retains its original internal name and OFL license.
